Q1:
SFML coords system works with X and Y, with the top-left corner being (0, 0) and +y is downwards and +x is right. 
I've worked with pygames so this wasn't any surprising.
However, the angle calculation must be rotated 90 degrees counterclockwise to account for the coordinate system.
